How to play

Out-expand and out-fight EvilDarthMonkey25 across 64 territories in 8 realms. Highest score after 20 rounds wins, or wipe out every enemy site.

Your turn

  • You get 5 moves per turn, plus bonus moves for dominance (below). The dots next to the round show what's left.
  • Claim an open site (1 move). You can claim in your own territories, or in empty territories that border one of yours.
  • Attack an enemy territory that borders yours (1 move). The battle plays out immediately.
  • A territory has one owner at a time. Press End turn › to pass early.

Battles

  • Attacker dice = your sites in territories bordering the target. Defender dice = their sites in the target plus their bordering territories. Minimum 2 dice each.
  • Highest dice are compared in pairs; ties go to the defender. Each lost pair removes one site from the loser.
  • The fight continues until one side runs out. If the attacker wins, they capture the territory with 1 site.

Scoring

  • +1 per site · +3 per territory you own · +5 per realm where you own at least 5 of 8 territories.
  • Ring dominance: own all 8 territories of a ring → +8 points and +1 move per turn.
  • Realm dominance: own all 8 territories of a realm → +10 points and +1 move per turn. Bonus moves cap at +3.

Controls

  • Hold the center button and drag to aim; keep dragging to roll around the globe and wrap to the far side. Release on a territory to enter it, release on a site to claim or attack.
  • Go back: tap outside the globe, use the breadcrumb at the top, press Esc, or roll to the far side (↩).
  • Exploded view: drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, click anything to inspect. Light lines are borders; dashed arcs cross the near pole and the far side.

THE LAST HOPE
